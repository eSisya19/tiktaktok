Overview

Your products, organised and ready to post

Sika gives small-business owners one central place for product photos, names, prices and details. Instead of searching through a phone gallery and posting every item individually, products remain organised and ready to reuse.

Products can be grouped into collections for launches, promotions, categories or seasonal stock. A complete collection can then be posted as a batch immediately, set to auto-post later or scheduled to auto-post daily. Once daily auto-posting is enabled, Sika’s rotation algorithm selects different products from the collection and in-app gallery, helping keep your WhatsApp Status and groups varied without repeating the same products every day.

Not the construction company. This Sika is a Zambian software product by Cryon Technologies Limited. It is unrelated to Sika AG, the Swiss construction-chemicals company.

Core features

From product catalogue to batch post

Sika turns a collection of products into a reusable posting workflow, saving the time normally spent rebuilding WhatsApp posts one by one.

01

One product catalogue

Save each product with its photo, name, price and details. Search, edit and reuse products without rebuilding every post.

02

Organised collections

Group related products into collections for categories, new arrivals, promotions or campaigns.

03

Batch posting

Post an entire collection together instead of selecting and publishing every product separately.

04

Auto-post later or daily

Choose a collection, its destination and when it should post. Sika can auto-post the batch later or manage it on a daily schedule for WhatsApp Status and selected groups.

05

Smart product rotation

For daily auto-posting, Sika’s rotation algorithm selects different products from your in-app gallery so your audience sees a changing mix of products.

06

Status and group management

Let Sika manage product posting to WhatsApp Status, selected groups or both, while you focus on running your business.

Responsible use

Automation still requires responsible use

Sika makes product posting easier, but account owners must follow WhatsApp’s rules. Contact only people who have consented, protect customer information and avoid misleading, repetitive or excessive posting.

Help

Frequently asked questions

Is Sika free?

Sika is promoted with a free way to get started. Features and limits may change, so check the official app.

Is Sika made in Zambia?

Sika is developed by Cryon Technologies Limited for the Zambian market.

How does daily auto-posting avoid repetition?

Sika uses a rotation algorithm to select different products from your chosen collection and in-app gallery, creating a changing mix of posts across days.

Do I need a new WhatsApp account?

No. Sika is designed to link with the account your business already uses.

Does Sika use WhatsApp’s end-to-end encryption?

Yes. Sika connects as an authorised WhatsApp linked device, so WhatsApp’s end-to-end encryption remains active when messages, media, Status updates and group posts are transmitted. The linked-device connection is between you—the customer—and Sika. Intended WhatsApp recipients can still see the content you choose to post or send.

Is this connected to Sika AG?

No. This Sika is a WhatsApp marketing software product.
