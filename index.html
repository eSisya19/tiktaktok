Overview

Built for everyday business promotion

Sika brings common WhatsApp marketing tasks into one place. Businesses can prepare content, organise audiences and schedule activity instead of repeating the same manual steps every day.

It is aimed especially at small businesses and independent sellers in Zambia that use WhatsApp to display products, maintain customer relationships and announce offers.

Not the construction company. This Sika is a Zambian software product by Cryon Technologies Limited. It is unrelated to Sika AG, the Swiss construction-chemicals company.

Getting started

From sign-in to your first campaign

  1. Open SikaVisit the official app and sign in with your phone number.
  2. Link WhatsAppFollow the on-screen linking instructions for your business account.
  3. Add audience and contentImport contacts or groups you are authorised to contact, then prepare your message.
  4. Choose when to publishSend immediately or schedule the campaign.

Responsible use

Respect customers and WhatsApp rules

Contact only people who have consented, provide an easy way to opt out, protect customer information and avoid misleading or excessive promotions. The account owner remains responsible for every campaign.
