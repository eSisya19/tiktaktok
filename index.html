What is Sika?

Sika is a WhatsApp marketing and automation tool designed to help small businesses stay visible, organise promotions and communicate with customers more consistently.

Our WhatsApp connection guaranteeSika guarantees that it will never ask for or store your WhatsApp password. It connects through WhatsApp’s linked-device process. Account owners must still follow WhatsApp’s policies because decisions about account restrictions are controlled by WhatsApp.

Overview

Your products, organised and ready to post

Sika gives small-business owners one central place for product photos, names, prices and details. Instead of searching through a phone gallery and posting every item individually, products remain organised and ready to reuse.

Products can be grouped into collections for launches, promotions, categories or seasonal stock. A complete collection can then be posted as a batch immediately or scheduled to auto-post later.

Not the construction company. This Sika is a Zambian software product by Cryon Technologies Limited. It is unrelated to Sika AG, the Swiss construction-chemicals company.

Core features

From product catalogue to batch post

Sika turns a collection of products into a reusable posting workflow, saving the time normally spent rebuilding WhatsApp posts one by one.

01

One product catalogue

Save each product with its photo, name, price and details. Search, edit and reuse products without rebuilding every post.

02

Organised collections

Group related products into collections for categories, new arrivals, promotions or campaigns.

03

Batch posting

Post an entire collection together instead of selecting and publishing every product separately.

04

Scheduled auto-posting

Choose a collection, its destination and a time. Sika automatically posts the product batch to WhatsApp Status or selected groups.

05

Flexible destinations

Choose WhatsApp Status, selected groups or both for each collection.

06

Individual product posts

Post a single product whenever a full collection is not needed.

Getting started

Build once, post repeatedly

  1. Add your productsSave each product’s photo, name, price and details in your catalogue.
  2. Create a collectionGroup the products that should be promoted together.
  3. Choose destinationsSelect WhatsApp Status, the groups you are authorised to contact or both.
  4. Batch post or automatePublish the collection immediately or schedule Sika to auto-post it later.

Responsible use

Automation still requires responsible use

Sika makes product posting easier, but account owners must follow WhatsApp’s rules. Contact only people who have consented, protect customer information and avoid misleading, repetitive or excessive posting.
